About

Built for the way students actually learn.

SQL is easier to understand when you can see what a query does. SQL Playground gives you a place to experiment, make mistakes, and read a clear explanation without setting up a database on your own machine.

  • Role-based dashboards

    Learners and administrators see the tools that match their role.

  • Secure accounts

    Passwords are hashed, and profile data stays with its owner.

  • Clear query feedback

    Explanations focus on the idea behind the error, not just the syntax.

FAQ

Questions students ask before starting.

Do I need to install anything?

No. SQL Playground runs in your browser. You only need an account and an internet connection.

Is it free for students?

Yes. Creating a learner account is free. The project is built for classroom practice.

What can I practice?

Start with SELECT, filtering, sorting, and joins against sample databases. More lessons will follow as the workspace grows.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can read and review pages on a phone. Writing longer queries is more comfortable on a tablet or laptop.

How do I get an account?

Choose Create account, fill in your details, and sign in with your username or email address.
